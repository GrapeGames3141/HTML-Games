Privacy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This page explains how The Arcade Shelf uses browser storage and services that receive information when you visit.

Browser storage

The arcade stores your last played game in your browser so it can show that game and vary the random selection. Individual games may also save settings, scores, or progress on your device. You can clear site data in your browser settings; doing so may remove saved progress and preferences.

Advertising

The arcade uses Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers and receive information such as your IP address, browser information, and interactions with ads to deliver, personalize, and measure advertising. Google may use information from your visits to this and other sites, subject to your choices and applicable settings.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, read the Google Privacy Policy, or manage ad personalization in Google My Ad Center. You can also manage cookies through your browser. Blocking cookies may affect site features or saved preferences.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Your browser connects to Cloudflare to receive the site; Cloudflare may process connection information such as your IP address and request details to deliver and protect its service. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
