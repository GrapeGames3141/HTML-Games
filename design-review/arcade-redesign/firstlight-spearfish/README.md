# First Light and Spearfish arcade cards

These are real, browser-only headless captures from the local source, selected for the arcade image cards. No generated or repainted assets were used.

- `firstlight-raw.png`: `firstlight/firstlight.html?test&area=stand&weapon=rifle`, with the HUD/menu hidden in the capture browser, a test-spawned feeding buck, late-morning light, and the `FL.cam()` framing API.
- `spearfish-raw.png`: `spearfish/spearfish.html?test&seed=7`, with the HUD/menu hidden in the capture browser, a calm nearest fish, and the `SF.stalk()` framing API.

The published card derivatives are `assets/arcade/firstlight.webp` and `assets/arcade/spearfish.webp`, each resized from its corresponding 1200×840 raw capture to 800×560 WebP.
